Week 10

Food & Restaurants

Order complex meals and ask about ingredients.

1001 Ein Tag in der Küche
NEURAL2-F
Speed
  1. Where does Anja work, and how does she get to the market?
  2. What does Paul do in the kitchen, and what does Anja do?
  3. What is on today's lunch menu, and how much does it cost?
  4. Why does Anja need to know every recipe exactly?
1002 Mittagessen in der Kantine
NEURAL2-F NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. What are the two main dishes on today's menu?
  2. Why doesn't Jonas want the Schnitzel, and why not the fish?
  3. What does Jonas order in the end?
  4. Why doesn't Lena want to sit at the big table by the window?
1003 Der Kochkurs
NEURAL2-F
Speed
  1. What dish is the group cooking today?
  2. Where are the ingredients, and what should everyone do first?
  3. What do the vegetarian guests get?
  4. What happens at nine o'clock, and what does everyone get at the end?
1004 Eine Torte für die Kollegin
NEURAL2-F NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. Why does Frau Klein need a cake?
  2. What does her colleague like, and what is important to remember?
  3. How many pieces does the large cake have, and how much does it cost?
  4. When will Frau Klein pick up the cake, and what does Herr Huber give her today?
1005 Mein erstes Weihnachten in Deutschland
NEURAL2-F
Speed
  1. Where does Stefan's family live, and how did Carmen and Stefan get there?
  2. What did Stefan's mother make on Christmas Eve, and why was Carmen surprised?
  3. What did Stefan's grandmother give Carmen, and what did Carmen give her?
  4. What is different about Christmas this year?
1006 Samstag auf dem Viktualienmarkt
NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. Who does Thomas go to the market with, and how do they get there?
  2. What happens at the fruit stall?
  3. What does Thomas buy at the butcher's, and what does Leo think about the cheese?
  4. Where do they eat at twelve o'clock, and what do they have?
This week focuses on adjective endings in the dative (mit dem frischen Salat, in einem kleinen Restaurant) and on pronouns (ihn/ihm, mich/mir). Each script is embedded directly in its matching lesson (1001 → Lesson 1, 1002 → Lesson 2, etc.).
