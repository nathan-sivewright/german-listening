Week 08

Health & Body / The ICE Commute

Explain an illness or injury at the Apotheke, and complain about a Zugverspätung.

0801 Ich bin krank
NEURAL2-F
Speed
  1. What symptoms does the speaker describe?
  2. What did the speaker do this morning?
  3. What is the speaker's plan for today?
  4. What does the speaker hope for tomorrow?
0802 Beim Apotheker
NEURAL2-F NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. What does the customer say is wrong?
  2. What two pieces of advice does the pharmacist give?
  3. Does the customer need a prescription?
  4. What should the customer do if it doesn't get better by tomorrow?
0803 Verspätung am Bahnhof
NEURAL2-F NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. What problem is announced at the station?
  2. What does the traveller ask the station worker?
  3. What does the station worker advise the traveller to do?
  4. What will happen to the traveller's original connection?
0804 Ein schlechter Tag
NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. What happened to the speaker's train this morning?
  2. What did the speaker do instead of waiting?
  3. What happened to the speaker in the afternoon?
  4. How does the speaker describe the whole day?
0805 Meine Woche
NEURAL2-F
Speed
  1. What happened to the speaker on Tuesday?
  2. What did the speaker do on Wednesday?
  3. What advice did the speaker receive?
  4. How does the speaker feel now, at the end of the week?
0806 Zwei Freunde erzählen
NEURAL2-F NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. What has been wrong with the first speaker this week?
  2. What advice did they receive?
  3. What happened to the second speaker on their commute?
  4. What do the two friends agree to do?
This week merges two grammar points — the Imperative and a Perfekt tense review — so scripts mix giving/quoting advice with narrating what happened. Each script is embedded directly in its matching lesson (0801 → Lesson 1, 0802 → Lesson 2, etc.).
