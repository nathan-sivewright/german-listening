Week 09

Shopping & Clothes

Describe items you want to buy or return.

0901 Im Büro und am Wochenende
NEURAL2-F
Speed
  1. What does Katrin usually wear to the office?
  2. Why does she sometimes wear a jumper at work?
  3. What does she wear at the weekend?
  4. What does she need to buy next week, and why?
0902 Ein Geschenk für Mia
NEURAL2-F NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. Whose birthday is it, and when?
  2. Why does Sophie want to buy a scarf?
  3. Why don't they buy the grey scarf or the red scarf?
  4. What do they decide to buy in the end, and who pays for what?
0903 Im Second-Hand-Laden
NEURAL2-F NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. Why does Herr Weber want to sell his clothes?
  2. What clothes has he brought with him?
  3. Why doesn't the shop want the shirts?
  4. How much money does he get in total, and how does he want to be paid?
0904 Durchsage im Kaufhaus
NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. What is on offer on the ground floor?
  2. What do you get free if you buy two shirts?
  3. What is special about children's shoes this week?
  4. Until when are the offers valid?
0905 Der Schrank ist zu voll
NEURAL2-F NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. Where are Jana and Tobias taking the clothes, and why there?
  2. Why does Tobias want to give away his green jumper?
  3. What does Jana keep, and what does she give away?
  4. What happens to Tobias's old jeans, and why?
0906 Opa erzählt
NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. How did the speaker's family usually get clothes when he was a child?
  2. How often did they buy new clothes, and where?
  3. What happened when he was ten years old?
  4. What does he find hard to understand about his granddaughter?
This week focuses on adjective endings in the accusative: what you buy, wear, try on or give away. Each script is embedded directly in its matching lesson (0901 → Lesson 1, 0902 → Lesson 2, etc.).
