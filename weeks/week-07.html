Speed

0703 My Office
NEURAL2-B
Speed
  1. What two items are on the speaker's desk?
  2. What problem does the speaker mention about the printer?
  3. How long has the speaker worked at this desk?
  4. Where does the speaker usually spend their lunch break?
0704 Job Interview
NEURAL2-F NEURAL2-B
Speed

  3. When could the candidate start?
  4. By when will the company respond?
0705 Writing My Application
NEURAL2-F
Speed
  1. What did the speaker do yesterday?
  2. What two documents does the speaker mention preparing?
  3. How many companies will receive the application?
  4. How does the speaker feel about the process?
0706 First Day at the New Job
NEURAL2-F
Speed
  1. What day is it for the speaker?
  2. What time does the core working hours (Kernarbeitszeit) actually start?
  3. What did the new boss do for the speaker?
  4. How did the speaker feel at the end of the day?
0702 and 0704 are the first two-speaker dialogues on this site — each voice stays consistent per speaker throughout, rather than one random voice for the whole script. Each script is now embedded directly in its matching lesson (0701 → Lesson 1, 0702 → Lesson 2, etc.) rather than left as a separate track.
